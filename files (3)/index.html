01 — Who We Are

More than a club.
A movement.

EcoTech Club is an official student-led organization at the National Polytechnic School of Constantine, dedicated to harnessing the power of technology in service of environmental sustainability.

We bring together passionate students from engineering, computer science, biology, and design to collaborate on real-world environmental challenges — from AI-powered climate monitoring to robotics for precision agriculture.
